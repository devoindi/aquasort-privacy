Privacy Policy — AquaSort

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  |  Package: com.indissrathi.aquasort

Indiss Rathi built AquaSort as a free, 100% offline puzzle game.

1. Information We Collect

AquaSort does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No name, email address, location, device identifier, advertising ID, or analytics data is collected.
  • The app does not use internet permissions and contains zero third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All game progress (unlocked levels, best move counts, star ratings, saved boards, and Settings preferences) is stored strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all locally stored data.

3. Device Permissions

AquaSort requests only android.permission.VIBRATE to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay, which can be toggled off in Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Because AquaSort collects no data of any kind, no personal information is collected from children under 13 or any other users.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: support.indiss@gmail.com
